## Ingredients

**Peppers:** Chocolate Habanero, Carolina Reaper

**Other:** Bell peppers, Onions, Tomatoes, Garlic, Black garlic, Cane sugar, Molasses, Distilled vinegar, Spices

## First Impressions

Strong heat right out of the bottle. The pepper blend hits immediately but doesn't overwhelm. The label art is striking too, very on-brand for a Venetian Snares collab.

## Taste

The black garlic is the standout. It rounds out the pepper heat with a deep, slightly sweet earthiness that pairs exceptionally well with tomato-based dishes. The molasses adds a subtle background sweetness that keeps it from being one-dimensional.

## Texture & Appearance

Deep reddish-brown color with good viscosity. Pours clean and clings well. Minimal separation in the bottle, which speaks to the quality of the small-batch production.

## Verdict

Current top pick. The pepper blend is dialed in and the black garlic elevates it beyond a straightforward hot sauce. Versatile enough for everyday use but complex enough to appreciate on its own.

Four bottles in and I'd keep buying it. The limited edition status is genuinely frustrating.

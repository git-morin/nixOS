# Ticketmaster

[2022-10 → present]{.badge .badge-ongoing}

I joined Ticketmaster in October 2022 as a software developer on a
platform team working on identity and access for business users.

Day-to-day my work spans:

- Backend services in Java and [Spring](https://spring.io/).
- Streaming and data pipelines ([Kafka](https://kafka.apache.org/),
  [Benthos](https://www.benthos.dev/), [Databricks](https://www.databricks.com/)).
- Platform and infrastructure ([Pulumi](https://www.pulumi.com/),
  [Prometheus](https://prometheus.io/), [Splunk](https://www.splunk.com/),
  [GitLab CI](https://docs.gitlab.com/ee/ci/)).
- Developer tooling and frontend contributions.

I also carry on-call, code review, and team-tooling responsibilities
alongside delivery work.
